Major Findings:
Resident records, household rosters, and purok lists are kept on paper and in spreadsheets, making searches slow and error-prone. The blotter is a handwritten logbook with inconsistent case numbering; producing an official copy means copying by hand. Welfare assistance requests are tracked ad hoc with no record of approved versus released amounts. Account resets currently require a personal visit; the barangay wants email-based self-service. Officials need an accountability trail (who changed what, when, from where) for audit purposes.
